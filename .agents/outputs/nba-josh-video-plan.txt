09 Ca pC ut C omp os it e — St ep by S te p

 1      Import Officers clip as base track
        Open CapCut → New Project → Add officers clip to main timeline first. This is your background
        layer.


 2      Loop officers to 15 seconds
        Tap clip → Copy → paste repeatedly until timeline hits 15 seconds. Trim last copy to fit exactly.


 3      Add Josh clip as overlay
        Tap + → Overlay → import Josh clip. Position him to the bottom-right of frame. Scale to fit.


 4      Blur the officers layer
        Select officers clip → Effects → apply soft gaussian blur. Pushes them into background, creates
        depth.


 5      Add motion blur to officers
        Select officers layer → Video Effects → search Motion Blur . Medium intensity. Makes the loop
        feel like real running energy.


 6      Colour grade the full scene
        Select all → Adjust : Brightness -10, Contrast +20, Saturation -15. Add a cinematic LUT
        (teal/orange) from CapCut filters.


 7      Add vignette
         Effects   → Vignette at 30–40%. Darkens corners, focuses eye on Josh.


 8      Export
        Tap Export → 1080p at 60fps . Save to camera roll. Done.
