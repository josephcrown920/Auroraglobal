🔥 Most Used Features (Ranked by Real Usage Behavior)

🥇 1. Templates / One-Tap Video Creation (MOST USED)

This is #1 by far.

* Users don’t want to edit → they want results instantly
* Tap template → insert clips → DONE

Why it dominates:

* Fastest path to viral content
* Zero skill required
* Built for trends (TikTok/Reels)

📊 Data signal:

* Entire market shifting to template-driven flows  

⸻

🥈 2. Auto Editing (AutoCut / Smart Editing)

* Upload clips → AI edits everything
* Adds:
    * Cuts
    * Effects
    * Music sync

💡 This replaces traditional editing completely

People want “finished video” not “timeline control”

⸻

🥉 3. Auto Captions (HIGH retention feature)

* Generates subtitles instantly
* Multi-language support

📈 Why it matters:

* Social videos without captions = lower engagement
* Huge for:
    * TikTok
    * Instagram
    * YouTube Shorts

👉 This is a must-have feature now

⸻

🔥 4. Background Removal / Green Screen AI

* Remove background with 1 click
* No studio needed

📊 Widely used because:

* Makes videos look “professional” instantly
* Enables storytelling without resources  

⸻

🎥 5. Text-to-Video / Image-to-Video (FAST GROWING)

* Type prompt → get full video

⚠️ Important:

* Not the MOST used yet
* But fastest growing curiosity feature

📊 Supported by:

* New AI video apps + Meta AI growth driven by video feeds  

⸻

🎛️ 6. Effects, Filters, Transitions (BUT…)

* Still heavily used
* BUT mostly via templates

💡 Insight:

Users don’t manually pick effects anymore
They choose pre-packaged styles

⸻

🧠 7. Voice AI (Text-to-Speech / Voiceovers)

* Used for:
    * Faceless content
    * Story videos
    * Explainers

📈 Growing fast due to:

* TikTok automation content

⸻

✂️ 8. Manual Editing (Least Important Now)

* Timeline editing
* Keyframes
* Advanced control

⚠️ Reality:

Only power users care about this

⸻

🎯 Where Users Actually Lean (This is KEY)

1) ⚡ Speed > Quality

* “Good enough in 10 seconds” beats “perfect in 10 minutes”

⸻

2) 🎬 Short-Form Content Creation

* Everything optimized for:
    * TikTok
    * Reels
    * Shorts

📊 Entire ecosystem pushing this behavior  

⸻

3) 🧩 Simplicity Over Control

From Reddit (real user behavior):

“You can jump in, throw together an edit… without hours”  

⸻

4) 🎨 Presets Over Creativity

Users prefer:

* “Make it look like this”
    instead of
* “Let me design everything”

⸻

5) 🔁 Repeatable Content (Loop Behavior)

People want:

* Generate → post → repeat
* Not spend hours crafting one video

⸻

6) 📱 Mobile-First ALWAYS

* Most usage happens on phones
* Desktop = secondary

⸻

🧠 Hidden Truth (Most Important Insight)

❗ AI generation is NOT the core feature

What wins is:

“Turn anything into a viral-ready video instantly”

Not:

“Generate video with AI”
