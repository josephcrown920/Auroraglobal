An affiliate program is probably the fastest way to grow Aurora once the product is stable.

For your product, I wouldn’t call them “affiliates.” I’d call them something that feels exclusive.

Some ideas:

* Aurora Partners ⭐ (my favorite)
* Aurora Creators
* Aurora Ambassadors
* Aurora Founding Creators
* Aurora Collective
* Aurora Insiders

What they get

Keep it simple.

* 30–40% commission on every sale
* Personal referral link
* Free Aura credits every month
* Early access to new features
* Their videos featured on Aurora’s homepage and socials
* Private Discord/community

What you get

Instead of paying for ads:

* 100 creators each making 3 videos/week
* Hundreds of TikToks and Reels showing Aurora
* Trust because creators are demonstrating the product
* Sales coming in without you creating every piece of content

That’s exactly how products like CapCut, ElevenLabs, and many AI tools spread.

⸻

Who to recruit first

Don’t chase million-follower creators.

Go after:

* Music artists (5k–100k followers)
* AI creators
* CapCut editors
* Music video editors
* TikTok creators
* YouTubers teaching AI
* Instagram reel creators

A creator with 20k engaged followers often converts better than someone with 2 million passive followers.

⸻

The DM

Keep it short.

Hey, I built Aurora—an AI platform that creates cinematic music videos, motion transfer, lip sync and identity-locked AI scenes in minutes.

I think your audience would genuinely enjoy it.

I’d love to invite you as one of our Founding Creators. You’ll get free credits plus recurring commission for anyone who joins through your link.

Interested?

⸻

Make it ridiculously easy

Create a page that says:

Become an Aurora Partner

Earn 35% recurring commission.

✅ Free credits

✅ Personal referral dashboard

✅ Featured on Aurora

✅ Early feature access

Apply in under 60 seconds

⸻

One thing I’d add once you launch: inside Aurora itself, put a button that says “Earn Free Aura.”

When users click it:

Invite a friend → they sign up → you both receive free Aura.

That turns every happy customer into someone promoting the product, even if they don’t consider themselves an affiliate.

If Aurora consistently produces the kind of cinematic, identity-locked results you’ve been building toward, that combination of referrals plus creator partnerships can become a much more scalable growth engine than relying on paid ads alone.
