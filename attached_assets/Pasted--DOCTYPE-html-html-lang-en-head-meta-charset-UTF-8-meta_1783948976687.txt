    <div class="section-header">
      <div class="section-num">07</div>
      <div class="section-title">CapCut Composite — Step by Step</div>
    </div>
    <div class="section-body">
      <div class="steps">
        <div class="step-item">
          <div class="step-circle">1</div>
          <div class="step-content">
            <div class="step-heading">Import Layer B (Officers) as base track</div>
            <div class="step-note">Open CapCut → New Project → Add the officers clip to the main timeline first. This is your background.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <div class="step-circle">2</div>
          <div class="step-content">
            <div class="step-heading">Loop the officers clip to 15 seconds</div>
            <div class="step-note">Tap the clip → <code>Copy</code> → paste it back-to-back until total length reaches 15 seconds. Or use CapCut's <code>Loop</code> feature if available.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <div class="step-circle">3</div>
          <div class="step-content">
            <div class="step-heading">Add Layer A (Josh) as overlay</div>
            <div class="step-note">Tap <code>+</code> → <code>Overlay</code> → import the Josh clip. Place it over the officers timeline. Scale and position him to the bottom-right of frame.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <div class="step-circle">4</div>
          <div class="step-content">
            <div class="step-heading">Apply blur to the officers layer</div>
            <div class="step-note">Select the officers clip → <code>Effects</code> → add a soft blur filter. This creates depth and the shallow DOF look, pushing them into the background.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <div class="step-circle">5</div>
          <div class="step-content">
            <div class="step-heading">Add motion blur to officers</div>
            <div class="step-note">Select officers layer → <code>Video Effects</code> → search <code>Motion Blur</code>. Apply at medium intensity to reinforce the running energy.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <div class="step-circle">6</div>
          <div class="step-content">
            <div class="step-heading">Colour grade the whole scene</div>
            <div class="step-note">Select all → <code>Adjust</code>: Brightness -10, Contrast +20, Saturation -15. Then add a <code>Cinematic</code> LUT filter from CapCut's filter library (look for teal/orange presets).</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <div class="step-circle">7</div>
          <div class="step-content">
            <div class="step-heading">Add vignette</div>
            <div class="step-note">Go to <code>Effects</code> → <code>Vignette</code>. Apply at 30–40% intensity. This darkens the corners and focuses attention on Josh.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <div class="step-circle">8</div>
          <div class="step-content">
            <div class="step-heading">Export at highest quality</div>
            <div class="step-note">Tap <code>Export</code> → select <code>1080p</code> at <code>60fps</code>. Save to camera roll.</div>
